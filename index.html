WHO WE ARE

Nazeen Welfare Foundation

Nazeen Welfare Foundation is a social welfare organization dedicated to supporting underprivileged individuals and communities through education, healthcare, food assistance, emergency relief, and community development initiatives. We work with compassion, transparency, and a commitment to creating meaningful and lasting social impact.

Registered under section 8 company, the foundation is guided by Founder & Director Zahid Akhtar / Nafis Ahmad, and works tirelessly across communities to make a difference where it matters most — “Together, we aim to spread hope, create opportunities, and change lives.”

Our Work

Nazeen Welfare Foundation runs several impactful programs that focus on education, food security, healthcare, emergency relief, and social welfare. Each initiative is designed to bring real, measurable change in the lives of the people we serve.

🎓

Education Support

Supporting students through educational materials, learning resources, and opportunities — including free books and study guides for children who need them most.

🍟

Food & Essential Distribution

Providing food, clothing, and essential items to individuals and families in need across our communities.

🏥

Healthcare Support

Promoting health awareness and supporting people facing healthcare-related challenges through outreach and guidance.

🚨

Emergency Relief

Providing timely assistance and essential support during emergencies and difficult situations, including cold-weather and night relief drives.

👩

Women & Child Welfare

Supporting the well-being, education, safety, and opportunities of women and children in the communities we serve.

🤝

Community Development

Conducting community-focused initiatives that promote awareness, participation, dignity, and social development.

Our Mission & Vision

Inspiring Change Through Education, Empowerment, and Compassion

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower underprivileged individuals and communities by providing meaningful support in education, healthcare, food security, emergency relief, and social welfare. We strive to serve with compassion, transparency, and responsibility while creating opportunities for a better future.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build an inclusive and compassionate society where every individual can live with dignity, access basic necessities, receive equal opportunities, and move towards a healthier, educated, and more secure future.

Our Commitment

  • Serving communities with sincerity and compassion
  • Maintaining full transparency in all our work
  • Using donated resources responsibly
  • Continuously expanding our efforts to reach those in need
  • Building a network of volunteers committed to social good
